The Journey of Authentic SINGLE ORIGIN TEA

The BRIAR Group’s single origin tea heritage dates back to 1928. Over generations, BRIAR has grown with a singular purpose: to provide an authentic, undiluted tea experience in every cup.

Our tea estates extend across the most exclusive South Indian plantation districts—from the Kannan Devan Hills in Kerala to the Nilgiri-Wayanad, Meghamalai, and Anamalai ranges in Tamil Nadu. Spanning over 23,000 acres, our gardens nurture tea bushes in mist-laden valleys where crisp mountain air and mineral-rich springs impart unmistakable flavor signatures.

FAQ: SINGLE ORIGIN TEAS

Learn why single origin teas offer an incomparable sensory experience compared to generic commercial blends.

Single Origin means that every single tea leaf inside your package comes exclusively from one specific plantation estate, without being mixed or blended with lower-grade teas from multiple sources. This preserves the pure terroir, elevation, and distinct microclimate character of that single mountain garden.
Most supermarket brands blend teas from dozens of different auctions, countries, and harvest years to minimize costs and standardize a generic taste. In contrast, BRIAR teas are harvested from our own estate gardens and packed fresh at our own factories, guaranteeing unadulterated freshness and purity.
Tea is an agricultural produce packed with delicate volatile oils, aroma compounds, and polyphenols. Over months on wholesale shelves, commercial teas oxidize and lose their vitality. Our direct garden-to-doorstep model delivers tea within weeks of plucking, retaining full antioxidant potency.
The BRIAR Group takes immense pride in sustainable agricultural stewardship across our 23,000 acres. We practice largely biological and natural pest control methods across our gardens, protecting forest wildlife corridors, and maintaining fair housing and healthcare for over 8,800 estate workers.
Yes, 100%. We cultivate, pluck, wither, ferment, and pack all teas in our own 7 state-of-the-art factories (including Sussex, Talliar, Injipara, and Venniar). We never buy third-party auction dust or repackage external tea.
